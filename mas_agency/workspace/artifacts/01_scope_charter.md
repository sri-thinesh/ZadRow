## 1. Executive Summary
SYNAPSE CORE is an autonomous neurotechnology health platform engineered to bridge direct neural telemetry with actionable cognitive enhancement. The platform ingests, processes, and visualizes multi-channel EEG signal streams, translating microvolt-level electrical brainwave activity into real-time cognitive metrics—focus index, mental fatigue, stress load, and neuroplastic recovery states—delivering clinical-grade clarity through an ultra-responsive digital interface.

## 3. Target Persona Profiles

### Persona 1: Elite Cognitive Operator (Knowledge Professionals & Founders)
- **Persona ID:** `PER-01`
- **Pain Points:**
  - Unpredictable afternoon mental fatigue and attention degradation.
  - Lack of objective, quantifiable metrics for deep-work cognitive states.
  - Subjective guesswork in optimizing daily intellectual output and recovery.
- **Jobs to be Done:**
  - Monitor continuous cognitive focus and flow state durations in real time.
  - Receive preemptive bio-telemetric alerts before entering acute cognitive exhaustion.
  - Review empirical post-session neuro-performance audits to optimize focus blocks.

### Persona 2: Neuro-Wellness Seeker & Biohacker
- **Persona ID:** `PER-02`
- **Pain Points:**
  - Reliance on lagging biological indicators (heart rate variability, sleep stages) rather than direct cortical activity.
  - Disjointed consumer EEG interfaces that lack clinical signal interpretability.
  - Difficulty correlating stress interventions (breathwork, meditation) with direct neural relaxation.
- **Jobs to be Done:**
  - Observe immediate Alpha/Theta band shifts during active meditation and stress-recovery sessions.
  - Track longitudinal cognitive baseline trends and neural reserve recovery over 30/90 days.
  - Verify neuro-modulation effectiveness through empirical brainwave telemetry logs.

### Persona 3: Performance Health Clinician & Neuro-Coach
- **Persona ID:** `PER-03`
- **Pain Points:**
  - High barrier to accessing continuous out-of-clinic patient neural monitoring.
  - Overwhelming raw EEG signal data without synthesized executive summary metrics.
  - Absence of client compliance tracking for prescribed neuro-entrainment protocols.
- **Jobs to be Done:**
  - Audit aggregated client cognitive strain indices across designated time windows.
  - Export standardized clinical-grade telemetry summaries for diagnostic and coaching review.
  - Establish baseline variance thresholds for cognitive decline and recovery rates.

## 4. Scope Boundaries

### In-Scope Deliverables (MVP Baseline)
1. **Real-time streaming neural telemetry visualization dashboard** with multi-band EEG wave monitors (Delta, Theta, Alpha, Beta, Gamma).
2. **Autonomous cognitive state classification engine** (Focus, Flow, Stress, Fatigue, Recovery Indices).
3. **Interactive neural topography / hemispheric activation telemetry widget**.
4. **Session-based neuro-biometric recording, playback, and temporal scrub controls**.
5. **Preemptive cognitive exhaustion alerting module** with threshold triggers.
6. **Neuro-feedback entrainment session launcher** with closed-loop pacing recommendations.
7. **Device connectivity & signal quality integrity indicator** (electrode impedance monitoring simulator).
8. **Exportable neural performance audit summaries** and session analytical exports.

### Strictly Out-of-Scope (Zero Scope-Creep Boundary)
1. **Invasive Brain-Computer Interface (BCI)** surgical hardware integration protocols.
2. **Direct trans-cranial electrical stimulation (tDCS/tACS)** hardware control firmware.
3. **HIPAA-governed diagnostic medical billing** and electronic health record (EHR) integrations.
4. **Full native mobile application builds** (iOS/Android native binaries outside responsive web).
5. **Third-party enterprise multi-tenant clinic billing management portals**.
